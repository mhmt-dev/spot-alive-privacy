Spot Alive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Türkçe

In short: Spot Alive does not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email, phone number or location. No account is needed. Your progress stays on your own device. The app shows ads, and the ad provider collects its own data.

1. What we collect

We collect no personal data ourselves. Your progress, stars and settings are kept in your device's local storage and are never sent to us.

2. Third-party services

The app uses the services below. They may process data under their own privacy policies:

ServicePurposePolicy
Google AdMobIn-app advertising policies.google.com/privacy
Google Play ServicesDistribution and stability policies.google.com/privacy

Ad providers may use the advertising identifier, device type and coarse location (country) to serve and measure ads.

3. Personalised ads and consent

Players in the European Union, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are shown a consent screen the first time the app opens. You may decline personalised ads; ads are then shown based on context instead. You can change your choice later from the privacy settings inside the app.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS the system asks for your permission before the advertising identifier is used. Declining changes nothing about how the game plays.

5. Children's privacy

Spot Alive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe your child has sent us data, contact us at the address below and we will take care of it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device only. Removing the app deletes it. We hold no copy.

7. Security

Because we collect no personal data, there is no user database that could leak. Communication with third-party services uses encrypted connections.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. When something important changes, the date on this page changes with it.

9. Contact

Questions and requests: merivoltd@gmail.com
