Common questions

I bought Remove Ads but the ads are still showing.
Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. If that does not help, email us the date of purchase.
I changed phones. Do I keep my progress?
Progress is stored on the device, so it does not move to a new phone. The Remove Ads purchase does move: tap Restore Purchases.
A difference looks impossible to find.
Tap the bulb for a hint. Every difference is measured before release, but if one still feels unfair, email us the level number and we will look at it.
Can I turn off the daily reminder?
Yes, in Settings inside the game, or in iOS Settings under Notifications.
How do I change my ad personalisation choice?
Settings inside the game has a privacy options entry where you can change your consent at any time.
